# Safety notes

Clip Lite stays on **your computer**.  
It does **not** upload your copies to the internet.

## Please remember

- The copy list can show things you copied.
- Turn on **Private mode** when you copy something private.
- Press **Clear history** when you want the list gone.

## What we lock down

- Long text files are saved only in a private folder on your account.
- “Clear history” also deletes those files.
- The app will not open files from weird places outside that folder.
- Size limits stop huge copies from using too much memory.
